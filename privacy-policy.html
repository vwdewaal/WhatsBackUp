Privacy Policy – WhatsBackUp

Last updated: February 27, 2026

WhatsBackUp is an offline viewer for WhatsApp chat exports. Your chat files stay on your device. We do not sell personal data.

Who We Are

Developer: Louis De Waal
Contact: Louis@dewaal.co
Jurisdiction: United Kingdom

Eligibility & WhatsApp Restrictions

WhatsBackUp is available to WhatsApp users worldwide but is subject to the same country and eligibility restrictions as WhatsApp. If WhatsApp is restricted or unavailable in your region, WhatsBackUp may not function as intended.

Information We Collect

  • Chat export files you choose to import (ZIPs and extracted content). These are stored locally on your device.
  • Crash diagnostics via Firebase Crashlytics (e.g., device model, OS version, app version, crash stack traces).
  • Purchase and entitlement data via RevenueCat for in-app purchase management.

How We Use Information

  • To import and display your chat history.
  • To improve stability and performance (Crashlytics).
  • To manage purchases and entitlements (RevenueCat).

Data Storage & Processing

  • Chat data remains on your device only.
  • We do not upload your chat content to any server.
  • WhatsBackUp currently has no cloud backup or upload feature.

Network Connections

The app does not upload chat content. However, the app does use network connections for:

  • Crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • In-app purchase validation (RevenueCat).

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash diagnostics.
  • RevenueCat — purchase and entitlement management.

Children’s Privacy

WhatsBackUp is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Choices

  • You can delete imported chats from within the app.
  • Uninstalling the app removes local data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will reflect changes.
